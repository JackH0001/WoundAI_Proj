WoundLite 民眾版隱私權政策草稿

修訂：2026-10-01；同意文案版本 2026-10-01.1。App 不需註冊，也不要求姓名、電話或 email。

您可選擇離線量測

不同意研究上傳時，照片、深度與手動圈選在本機處理；量測流程不呼叫雲端辨識。照片與深度側檔以 AES-GCM 加密，金鑰在 Keychain。量測值、日期與輪廓目前存於沙箱中的 JSON，並非另以 AES 加密。刪除本機紀錄會一併刪除其影像與深度檔，不代表雲端副本也已刪除。

同意研究上傳時

目前 iOS 上傳影像、安裝假名代碼、同意版本，以及可用的 LiDAR 深度、信心圖與相機參數。影像重新編碼，但不能移除像素裡的姓名、臉部或證件。自動人臉檢查受部署設定及辨識限制影響，不能保證完全去識別。

研究儲存成功取得影像代碼後，您修改的輪廓可另行回傳。現行 iOS 呼叫未附上後端可選的 measured 欄位；不能宣稱所有本機量測值都已上傳。這些影像、輪廓與深度可能揭露健康資訊。

假名代碼與連線資料

anon_id 是隨機安裝代碼，不是 Apple ID、IDFA 或 IDFV，但會串聯同一安裝的上傳與限流紀錄。因此本版隱私宣告採「資料有關聯」，包含裝置識別代碼及健康資料；沒有帳號不等於完全匿名。

後端限流檔記錄 IP 雜湊與該代碼。IP 雜湊不等於保證不可反推，且不排除 Google Cloud 等基礎設施保留原始連線日誌。重新安裝或遺失代碼可能使舊資料難以定位。

停止上傳與刪除請求

設定頁關閉研究上傳可停止後續新上傳；已送出或進行中的請求不因此自動撤銷。目前 iOS 尚無一鍵刪除雲端研究資料的按鈕。後端已有刪除端點，但不能把端點存在當成使用者已可操作。請先透過聯絡管道提出申請；發布前須確認定位代碼、身分驗證與回覆方式。

用途及適用範圍

資料供雲端輪廓辨識、經同意的量測精度研究與模型改進，並作安全與流量管理；不用於廣告或跨 App 追蹤。App 未提供診斷；產品法規分類仍須專業評估。本版尚未完成 App Attest 與正式研究治理，暫不開放真實民眾研究上傳。

雲端服務、保存與測試限制

雲端功能由部署於 Google Cloud 的後端處理。應用程式層的安全措施不代表雲端基礎設施不會產生連線或存取日誌；營運方仍須確認日誌欄位、存取者、地區與保存期限。

目前示範測試僅使用合成或模擬資料。正式研究尚待 IRB 與儲存治理完成，不能由這份政策推論已核准收案。示範服務若採暫存容器，本機以外的紀錄可能隨重新部署或執行個體回收消失；請勿作為病歷的唯一保存處。

正式研究資料與稽核資料的保存期限、刪除例外及執行責任，須由營運方依核定計畫逐項確認後公布；本草稿不承諾尚未驗證的刪除時程或永久保存。

WoundLite Privacy Policy — Draft

Revised 2026-10-01; consent text version 2026-10-01.1. No registration, name, phone or email is required.

You can choose offline measurement

When research upload is declined, the measurement flow processes photos, depth and manually traced outlines locally without cloud segmentation. Photos and depth sidecars use AES-GCM with a Keychain key. Measurements, dates and outlines are currently sandboxed JSON, not separately AES-encrypted. Deleting a local record removes its image and depth file, not any cloud copies.

When you consent to research upload

The current iOS client sends the image, installation pseudonym and consent version, plus available LiDAR depth, confidence maps and camera parameters. Re-encoding does not remove faces, names or documents within the pixels. Face checking depends on deployment configuration and detection limits and cannot guarantee de-identification.

After successful research storage returns an image identifier, edited outlines can be sent separately. The current iOS call does not supply the backend's optional measured field; not every local measurement is uploaded. Images, outlines and depth may reveal health information.

Pseudonyms and connection information

anon_id is a random installation identifier, not Apple ID, IDFA or IDFV. It links uploads and rate-limit records from the same installation. The privacy declaration therefore marks this data as linked and includes device identifiers and health information. Having no account does not make the data fully anonymous.

Application rate-limit files store an IP hash and the installation identifier. Hashing does not guarantee that an IP cannot be inferred and does not exclude raw connection logs at infrastructure providers such as Google Cloud. Reinstalling or losing the identifier may make old data difficult to locate.

Stopping uploads and requesting deletion

Turning off research upload in Settings stops subsequent new uploads; it does not automatically recall requests already sent or in progress. The current iOS app has no one-tap cloud deletion button. A backend deletion endpoint exists, but this does not constitute a usable in-app flow. Contact the operator first; before publication, the identifier-recovery, verification and response process must be confirmed.

Purpose and availability

Processing supports cloud outlines, consented measurement research/model improvement, security and rate limiting, not advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not provide diagnosis; regulatory classification requires professional assessment. App Attest and formal research governance remain incomplete, so real public research uploads are not yet open.

Cloud processing, retention and test limits

Cloud functions use a backend deployed on Google Cloud. Application-level safeguards do not establish that infrastructure produces no connection or access logs. The operator must verify log fields, access, processing regions and retention.

Demonstration testing is limited to synthetic or phantom data. Formal research awaits IRB and storage-governance readiness; this policy does not establish approval to enroll participants. A demo using ephemeral container storage may lose server records on replacement or redeployment. Do not use it as the sole copy of a clinical record.

Before research use, the operator must publish verified retention periods, deletion exceptions and responsibilities under the approved protocol. This draft does not promise unverified deletion deadlines or permanent storage.

Contact, changes and publication prerequisites

Existing contact: jack.hou@gmail.com (awaiting owner confirmation). Do not email wound photos or patient identifiers; request a secure channel first.

Publication requires the responsible entity, approved service/research scope, providers and processing regions, retention periods and rights-request process. This code-based draft makes no claim of regulatory clearance or medical-device classification.
