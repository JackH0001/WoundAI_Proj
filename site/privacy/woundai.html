WoundAI 醫療版隱私權政策草稿

修訂：2026-10-01。供有權限的照護／測試人員使用，帳號由管理者開通；App 不提供自行註冊。

本機資料

病患姓名、病歷號與簽名欄位在本機加密；傷口照片及深度側檔以 AES-GCM 儲存，金鑰位於裝置 Keychain。並非整個資料庫皆有應用層加密：量測數值與部分索引仍由 App 沙箱及系統資料保護管理。

這些病患身分欄位不包含在正常雲端量測請求內，但影像或自由文字可能自行含有識別內容。請避免臉部、證件與名牌入鏡。WD 代碼是可關聯同一個案的假名，不能宣稱完全匿名。

雲端量測與研究上傳是不同步驟

呼叫雲端分析時，傷口影像會離開裝置供辨識，即使未勾選訓練同意亦然。臨床照護同意取得有效 receipt 時，後端可能保存該次照護影像；是否實際保存以後端回覆為準。登入帳號、角色、個案代碼與操作紀錄會在後端使用。

訓練同意另外控制研究標註與資料使用；有權限者可送出修正輪廓、組織遮罩、量測值，以及選擇補傳 LiDAR 深度、信心圖、相機參數與拍攝裝置資訊。撤回研究同意不代表已保存的照護／稽核紀錄同時刪除。

保存、撤回與稽核

本機設有結案 90 天後的影像清理流程，須由 App 執行；不保證 App 關閉時在到期瞬間清除。進行中個案及時間軸量測紀錄不套用同一影像期限。

可由照護團隊提出查詢、更正或撤回研究同意。後端對撤回資料做排除後續訓練／匯出的處理；已訓練模型的個別樣本影響不等同可直接刪除的一筆檔案。

稽核使用雜湊鏈。保留政策與不可逆鎖定是不同狀態，目前不能概括宣稱所有部署均為 WORM／不可竄改；正式收案前須讀回實際桶政策並驗證鎖定。示範容器儲存不具此保證。

使用目的與傳輸

資料用於量測、照護紀錄、經同意的研究與安全稽核，不用於廣告或跨 App 追蹤。雲端處理會涉及服務供應商，不能宣稱不向任何第三方處理者提供資料。Release 預設後端使用 HTTPS，未允許任意網域明文傳輸。

雲端服務、保存與測試限制

雲端功能由部署於 Google Cloud 的後端處理。應用程式層的安全措施不代表雲端基礎設施不會產生連線或存取日誌；營運方仍須確認日誌欄位、存取者、地區與保存期限。

目前示範測試僅使用合成或模擬資料。正式研究尚待 IRB 與儲存治理完成，不能由這份政策推論已核准收案。示範服務若採暫存容器，本機以外的紀錄可能隨重新部署或執行個體回收消失；請勿作為病歷的唯一保存處。

正式研究資料與稽核資料的保存期限、刪除例外及執行責任，須由營運方依核定計畫逐項確認後公布；本草稿不承諾尚未驗證的刪除時程或永久保存。

WoundAI Clinical Privacy Policy — Draft

Revised 2026-10-01. For authorized care or test personnel. Administrators provision accounts; no self-registration is provided.

On-device data

Patient-name, medical-record-number and signature fields are encrypted locally. Photos and depth sidecars use AES-GCM with keys in device Keychain. This is not whole-database encryption: measurements and some indexes rely on the app sandbox and system data protection.

Those patient identity fields are not part of normal cloud measurement requests, but photos or free text can themselves contain identifying material. Keep faces, badges and identity documents out of images. A WD code links records for a case and is pseudonymous, not fully anonymous.

Cloud measurement and research are separate

Cloud analysis sends the wound image off-device even without training consent. A valid care-consent receipt may permit backend retention of the care image; the backend response indicates whether persistence occurred. Login identity, role, case code and operational records are processed by the server.

Training consent separately governs research annotation and use. Authorized users may submit corrected outlines, tissue masks and measurements, and optionally depth maps, confidence maps, camera parameters and capture-device information. Research withdrawal does not automatically delete care or audit records.

Retention, withdrawal and audit

The app has a local image cleanup routine for cases closed for 90 days. It must execute to perform cleanup; deletion is not guaranteed at the deadline while the app is closed. Active-case images and timeline measurements follow different rules.

Request access, correction or research withdrawal through the care team. The backend excludes withdrawn samples from subsequent training/export; an individual sample's influence in an already-trained model is not a directly removable file.

The audit uses a hash chain. Retention policy and irreversible locking are distinct. Not all deployments can be described as WORM or tamper-proof. Formal enrollment requires verified readback and locking; ephemeral demo storage provides no such guarantee.

Purposes and transport

Data supports measurement, care records, consented research and security audit, not advertising or cross-app tracking. Cloud service providers process data; this is not a promise of no third-party processors. The Release default uses HTTPS and does not allow arbitrary cleartext domains.

Cloud processing, retention and test limits

Cloud functions use a backend deployed on Google Cloud. Application-level safeguards do not establish that infrastructure produces no connection or access logs. The operator must verify log fields, access, processing regions and retention.

Demonstration testing is limited to synthetic or phantom data. Formal research awaits IRB and storage-governance readiness; this policy does not establish approval to enroll participants. A demo using ephemeral container storage may lose server records on replacement or redeployment. Do not use it as the sole copy of a clinical record.

Before research use, the operator must publish verified retention periods, deletion exceptions and responsibilities under the approved protocol. This draft does not promise unverified deletion deadlines or permanent storage.

Publication requires the responsible entity, approved service/research scope, providers and processing regions, retention periods and rights-request process. This code-based draft makes no claim of regulatory clearance or medical-device classification.
